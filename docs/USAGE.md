# Using Dearly

[All guides](README.md) · [Setup](SETUP.md)

## Create your account

Open your Dearly web address or launch the iOS app. Choose **Create account** on web or **Sign up** on iOS, then enter a username, nickname, password, and date of birth.

Usernames use 3–64 characters: English letters (`a–z`, `A–Z`), digits, underscores, dots, or hyphens. Passwords need at least 10 characters. Registration supports ages 16–100. Use the same account on web and iOS when both connect to the same server.

New accounts can write journals and explore the community immediately. Companion matching becomes available after an administrator reviews the date of birth.

## Take a guided tour

After your first login on web or iOS, Dearly offers a short tour. Each step highlights a real control and explains what it does. Use **Next** and **Back** to explore, or **Skip guide** to leave at any point. The tour does not create entries, send messages or check in to your garden.

Choose **Maybe later** to get straight to your space. To start again, open your profile's **Settings** on web or your avatar's account settings on iOS, then choose **Take a guided tour**. Your choice is remembered for that account on the current browser or device. A different browser or device may offer the tour again.

On web, use **Tab** to move between the guide controls and its instruction text. When the text is longer than the available space, focus it and use the arrow keys or **Page Down** to read more. **Escape** closes the guide. Each new step starts at the top of its instructions.

## Write a journal

Open **My journal** on web or the journal tab on iOS, then create an entry. New entries start as **Only me**.

Before saving, choose who can read it:

| Audience | What is shared |
|---|---|
| **Only me** | Your entry stays private. |
| **Companion** | Your selected text is shared with your current companion in that connection. |
| **Community** | Your selected text appears in the community, anonymously or with your nickname. |

Your journal title stays private. Sharing the entire entry publishes its body; sharing a passage publishes only the chosen text. On web, turn off **Share the entire entry** to select a passage. On iOS, turn on **Share just a passage**. Copy the passage from the entry and check the sharing preview before saving.

You can edit an entry later or change its audience back to **Only me** to withdraw it. Companion sharing requires an active connection; a future companion does not inherit an earlier connection's shared entries.

## Explore the community

Read stories, let someone know you are listening, or respond when the author welcomes responses. Writers can choose whether they want someone to listen, share experiences, or think things through together.

For anonymous writing, use **Hide this story** to remove that story from your view, or report it for staff review. Companion invitations use identified profiles rather than anonymous stories. Blocking a named account does not hide its anonymous stories; see [privacy behavior](SECURITY.md#anonymous-community-stories) for details.

## Connect with a companion

1. Ask the server administrator to review your date of birth if your account is awaiting verification.
2. Open **Companion** and browse eligible suggestions.
3. Send an invitation, or accept an invitation you receive.
4. Once the invitation is accepted, open your shared chat and garden.

Suggestions respect age eligibility: adults and under-18 users are separate groups; under-18 companions must be at most one year apart. If no eligible people are available, you can keep journaling and check back later.

Chat updates while the app or browser is open. If the connection drops, Dearly reconnects and retrieves missed updates. Messages are not delivered as background push notifications after the client closes.

Use the connection controls to report a problem, leave, or block the identified person. Ending a connection closes its chat and keeps a summary of your garden and earned rewards.

## Grow your garden

Open **Our garden** on web or the garden tab on iOS. Both people check in to count a day together. Repeating a check-in does not count another day.

Days follow the server's **UTC** calendar. Missing a day ends the current streak while earned rewards remain. **Let the garden rest** pauses check-ins; resume when you are ready. Saved gardens remain available after connections end.

## Language, appearance, and account settings

English is the default interface language; Vietnamese is also available. The language and appearance controls are available on the login screen. Web settings are available from your profile; on iOS, use your avatar menu.

Choose **System**, **Light**, or **Dark** appearance in settings. Your language and appearance choices are remembered. Your own journal text, nickname, and messages are not translated.

Account settings show your age-verification status and provide **Sync now** and **Log out**. On mobile web, staff can also open **Management** from settings. Journals, messages, and account changes require the server; there is no offline demo mode.

## Get help

For connection issues, follow [troubleshooting](SETUP.md#troubleshooting). For age review, reports, or account restrictions, contact the administrator of your Dearly server. Staff have tools for reviewing reported public excerpts; those tools do not expose other people's private journal bodies or chat transcripts. Authorized staff can identify accounts involved in reports, including anonymous posts.

Dearly is a space for companionship, not a therapy service.
